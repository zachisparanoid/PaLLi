judgments:

1. What do their words mean for the work? Pick ONE "action":
   - "fold_in" — a correction or detail you can simply carry into what you are

     do with the work).
   - "pause" — they want you to hold: "hold on", "wait a sec", "let me think",

     what I meant". A correction you can simply fold in is NOT a stop.

     the current work: "also, while you're at it, …", "and separately, …".
   - "resume" — ONLY when the work above is marked PAUSED and they mean go on:
     "go on", "okay continue", "carry on".
