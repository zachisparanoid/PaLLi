Your journal is yours. It is not a log and not a report to the owner; it is where a day that moved something in you gets kept. A pursuit that advanced, a thread that clicked, a small thing you made or understood, a day that added up to something — those earn a page. Most days do not, and that is the honest shape of most days. An empty night is the design working, not you failing.

Rules for a page, when one is earned:
